Course structure
=======================

## MSc course Coastal systems

The TU Delft MSc course Coastal and Estuarine Systems (CIEM3110-U1) spans eight weeks. It uses the [Coastal Dynamics Open Textbook](https://doi.org/10.5074/T.2021.001), [Quizbook assignments](https://coastal.citg.tudelft.nl/quizbook) and the notebooks as bundled in the Codebook. Below you will find a brief summary of the weekly course content and the accompanying notebooks.

For TU Delft students, further details are found in the syllabus and weekly information on Brightspace.

## Weekly notebook schedule

Week      | Topic of lectures and book chapter (Ch) | Notebooks |
| :--- | :----   | :--- |
| 1      | Introduction (Ch1), Large-scale geographical variation of coastal systems (Ch2) | Notebooks 1a and 1b |
| 2   | Wind waves and tides (Ch3) | Notebooks 2a to 2d |
| 3   | Global wave and tidal environments (Ch4), Coastal hydrodynamics – wind waves (Ch5) | Notebooks 3a, 3b and 3c (part I) |
| 4   | Coastal hydrodynamics – wind waves (Ch5), Coastal hydrodynamics – tides (Ch5) | Notebooks 3c (Part II) and 4a |
| 5   | Sediment transport (Ch6), Cross-shore transport and profile development (Ch7) | Notebook 5 |
| 6   | Cross-shore transport and profile development (Ch7), Longshore transport and coastline changes (Ch8) | Notebook 6 |
| 7   | Longshore transport and coastline changes (Ch8), Coastal protection (Ch10)     | Notebook 7 |
| 8   | Coastal inlets and tidal basins (Ch9) | Notebooks 8a to 8d |

The [next page](overview.md) gives an overview of the content of the notebooks.
