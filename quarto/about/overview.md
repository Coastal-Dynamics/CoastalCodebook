Notebook overview
=======================

The Coastal Codebook consists of a series of Jupyter notebooks, supplementing the [Coastal Dynamics Open Textbook](https://doi.org/10.5074/T.2021.001). The table below provides an overview of all the notebooks and the corresponding chapters of the notebook.

## Topics per notebook

N## Topics per notebook

| Name | Topic | Related textbook chapter(s) |
|:---|:---|:---|
| 1a_tectonic_classification | On tectonic classification of coasts | Ch2 – Large-scale geographical variation of coastal systems |
| 1b_process-based_classification | On process-based classification of coasts | Ch2 – Large-scale geographical variation of coastal systems |
| 2a_pre_knowledge_waves | To test your knowledge on the dispersion relationship, not needed if you master your Waves course material | Ch3 – Wind waves and tides |
| 2b_wave_dispersion_and_grouping | Practice a bit more with wave dispersion and study wave grouping (largely known from Waves, repeated in this week's lectures) | Ch3 – Wind waves and tides |
| 2c_generation_tide | A review of the origin of the tide (differential pull of the sun and moon) as discussed in this week's reading material and lectures, handy if you find this a difficult topic | Ch3 – Wind waves and tides |
| 2d_tidal_constituents | To get to know the tide, tidal propagation and tidal constituents | Ch3 – Wind waves and tides |
| 3a_tidal_environments | Discover the four different tidal environments | Ch4 – Global wave and tidal environments |
| 3b_wave_asymmetry_and_skewness | Recreate the figures in the book about skewed and asymmetric waves | Ch5 – Coastal hydrodynamics – wind waves |
| 3c_radiation_stresses_and_wave_forces | Cross-shore distribution of cross-shore and alongshore wave forces | Ch5 – Coastal hydrodynamics – wind waves |
| 4a_bound_long_wave | Understand the generation mechanism of the bound long wave | Ch5 – Coastal hydrodynamics – wind waves |
| 4b_shallow_water_tides | Discover overtides and compound tides in shallow water and learn about tidal analysis and prediction | Ch5 – Coastal hydrodynamics – tides |
| 5_coastal_classification_and_beach_states | On process-based classification and beach states for Brazilian beaches | Ch4 – Global wave and tidal environments; Ch7 – Cross-shore transport and profile development |
| 6_cross_shore_transport | On cross-shore sediment transport using velocity moments and shape functions | Ch7 – Cross-shore transport and profile development |
| 7_alongshore_transport | On alongshore sediment transport featuring the CERC formula, $(S-\phi)$-curves and coastline modeling | Ch8 – Longshore transport and coastline changes |
| 8a_escoffier_curve | Learn about inlet stability with the aid of the Escoffier curve | Ch9 – Coastal inlets and tidal basins |
| 8b_kreeke_robaczewska | Investigate tide-induced residual sediment transport following Van de Kreeke and Robaczewska | Ch9 – Coastal inlets and tidal basins |
| 8c_equilibrium_concentration | Get to know the difference between the equilibrium and lagged response of suspended sediment | Ch9 – Coastal inlets and tidal basins |
| 8d_interventions | Explore various interventions and (human-induced) changes in tidal inlet/basin systems | Ch9 – Coastal inlets and tidal basins |
